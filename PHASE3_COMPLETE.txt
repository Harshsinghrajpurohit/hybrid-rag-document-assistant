KEY FEATURES IMPLEMENTED
================================================================================

BM25 Keyword Search
  - Exact-match retrieval using rank-bm25
  - Handles acronyms and rare terms well
  - Fast O(n) scoring

Dense Semantic Search
  - Vector similarity from ChromaDB
  - Same Ollama model as Phase 2 (nomic-embed-text)
  - 768-dimensional embeddings

Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF)
  - Formula: 1 / (k + rank) where k=60
  - Combines sparse + dense fairly
  - No score normalization needed
  - Industry standard (TREC proven)

Hybrid Benefits
  - BM25: Catches exact keywords, acronyms, rare terms
  - Dense: Catches semantic meaning, paraphrasing
  - Combined: Best of both worlds

================================================================================
DESIGN DECISIONS
================================================================================

Why BM25?
  - Lightweight and already installed
  - Deterministic and fast
  - Industry standard for full-text search
  - No neural network overhead

Why RRF?
  - No score normalization required
  - Fair combination of different rankers
  - Empirically proven (TREC competitions)
  - k=60 is well-researched and robust

Why Dependency Injection?
  - Enables testing with mocks
  - Decouples services
  - Clear dependencies visible in constructor

Why Fetch 2xK Before Fusion?
  - Reduces ranking noise
  - Improves fusion quality
  - Final top-K selected from larger set
